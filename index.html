03 — About

A curious designer who likes solving other people's problems.

Hi, I'm Shrabin — a UI/UX designer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I'm a multidisciplinary designer with a curious mind: I enjoy meeting new people and understanding how they solve problems, and I focus on ethical, considered design that gives life to an idea digitally.

Over six years I've worked across web, mobile, e-commerce and branding — moving from research all the way through to production, picking up front-end development and motion work along the way. I lean on a background in photography and videography to bring an extra point of view into my design work.

I'm currently a UI/UX Designer at Fusemachines Nepal, and I'm always open to a good conversation about a hard product problem.
