RENTICA

Rentica is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace, asking connections through community, creating new experiences, maintaining sustainable solutions, and coming together to help one another and the planet.

Type: Multi-category Rental Marketplace App & Web Platform

Project Scope: End-to-end UX/UI design (Research, Information Architecture, Wireframing, Visual Design, and Prototyping)

The Problem

People frequently need specific items (tools, event gear, electronics, furniture) for short-term use, but buying them brand new is expensive, wasteful, and cluttered. Meanwhile, owners have valuable idle items sitting around that could generate passive income.

Objectives

Rentica connects item owners with people looking to rent items safely, affordably, and sustainably within their community.

Design Process

Research & Discovery

To understand user behaviors, pain points, and motivations in the rental market, qualitative and quantitative research methods were applied.

Target Audience

  • Renters: Students, young professionals, event planners, and DIYers looking for temporary use of goods without high upfront costs.
  • Owners: Individuals or small businesses looking to monetize their underutilized assets safely.

Competitor Analysis

Evaluated existing peer-to-peer rental platforms to identify gaps in trust, transparency of pricing (hidden fees), and convenience of pickup/delivery logistics.

User Persona

  • Name: Alex, 26 (The Urban Renter)
  • Goal: Wants to rent a camera and lighting gear for a weekend project without spending thousands to buy.
  • Pain Points: Hard to verify the condition of items, complex checkout processes, and lack of clear insurance or security deposits.

Information Architecture & User Flow

Core User Journey

  1. Discovery: Landing page / Explore items by category (Electronics, Furniture, Tools, Fashion).
  2. Search & Filter: Filtering by location, price range, availability dates, and ratings.
  3. Item Details: Checking specs, owner ratings, rental terms, and daily/weekly pricing.
  4. Booking & Checkout: Selecting dates, choosing delivery or pickup, security deposit handling, and payment.
  5. Post-Rental: Reviewing the item and owner experience.

Wireframing & Ideation

Low-fidelity sketches and wireframes were drafted to map out core screen layouts, emphasizing a clean search bar, prominent category icons, and easy date-picker selection.

Iterations focused on minimizing cognitive load during checkout and making safety/trust badges (verified users, secure payments) clearly visible on item cards.

Visual Design & UI

Color Palette

Clean neutrals paired with a vibrant accent color (warm orange/amber) to signify trust, energy, and action.

Primary (Accent):

#FF6B35#10A56B

Warm Amber / Emerald Teal — conveys energy, warmth, and trust for marketplace transactions.

Neutral Dark:

#1C293B

Charcoal / Slate — used for primary typography, headers, and high-contrast UI elements.

Neutral Light:

#F8FAFC#F1F5F9

Off-White / Soft Gray — provides clean background fill for card components and screen sections.

Feedback Colors:

#22C55E#EAB308#EF4444
  • Success: Soft Green — order confirmation, available stock, verified badge.
  • Warning / Alert: Muted Yellow — low stock, pending approval.
  • Error: Crimson Red — out of stock, payment errors, cancelled rentals.

Typography

Modern, highly readable sans-serif font optimized for mobile scanning.

Font Family: Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans (sans-serif)

Scale & Hierarchy:

  • H1 / Hero Title: 32px – Bold (Page Titles, Banner Headings)
  • H2 / Section Title: 24px – Semi-Bold (Category Headings, Product Section Headers)
  • H3 / Card Header: 18px – Medium (Item Names, Profile Titles)
  • Body Main: 16px – Regular (Descriptions, Reviews, Rental Terms)
  • Caption & Microcopy: 12px–14px – Regular/Medium (Price tags, badges, availability dates)

UI Components & Cards

Item Cards: Includes high-resolution product preview, item title, location radius (e.g., “Within 3 km”), daily rate badge, and owner rating star indicator.

Badges & Tags:

  • Verified Owner: Blue or Green checkmark badge to build trust.
  • Security Deposit: Transparent tag indicating if a deposit is required upfront.

Sticky CTA Bar: A fixed bottom navbar on mobile/desktop product pages displaying total cost per day and a primary action button (“Rent Now”).

Key Screens Designed

Header / Navigation:

  • Brand logo (Rentica), Search Bar with auto-complete (Location + Item Category), and Profile/Notifications icons.

Category Carousel / Grid:

  • Quick filter chips: Electronics, Cameras, Tools, Furniture, Party & Events, Sports Gear.

Hero Banner:

  • “Rent Anything Nearby” — clear value proposition with a quick-search callout.

Featured / Near You Section:

  • Grid view of top-rated items available immediately in the user’s vicinity.

Product Detail Page (PDP)
Visual Gallery: High-definition carousel showing the item from multiple angles and in real use.
Item Information Panel:

  • Title, Owner Avatar + Name + Rating (e.g., “★ 4.9 (32 rentals)”).
  • Condition tag (e.g., Like New, Gently Used).

Booking & Calendar Selection:

  • Interactive date-range picker for selecting rental start and end dates.
  • Pickup vs. Delivery option toggle.

Price Breakdown Card:

  • Base rental cost (e.g., $25/day × 3 days = $75)
  • Service / Cleaning fee
  • Refundable Security Deposit
  • Total Amount Due

Owner Guidelines & Deposit Policy: Expandable accordion detailing pickup rules, return conditions, and loss/damage coverage terms.

Booking & Checkout Flow
Step 1: Reservation Summary: Review selected dates, pickup/delivery address, and identity verification status.
Step 2: Security & ID Verification: Quick upload prompt for government ID (for first-time renters).
Step 3: Payment Method: Support for digital wallets, credit cards, and direct bank transfers.
Step 4: Confirmation Screen: Summary map showing pickup location pin, booking ID, and direct chat link with the item owner.

Outcome & Learnings

Impact: Rentica provides an intuitive, trust-driven ecosystem that encourages sustainable consumption through renting over buying.

Key Takeaway: Balancing transparency in pricing (especially handling security deposits and rental periods clearly) is vital to reducing cart abandonment in peer-to-peer marketplaces.

Iterations focused on minimizing cognitive load during checkout and making safety/trust badges (verified users, secure payments) clearly visible on item cards.
